Privacy Policy Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 2026
WordPlayLeague respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information used within the WordPlayLeague service.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how users can request access to or deletion of their data.
Information We Collect
WordPlayLeague collects only the information necessary to operate puzzle leagues and track scores.
This may include:
• Slack workspace identifiers
• Slack display names or usernames
• Player names entered during league setup
• Puzzle scores submitted by users
• League configuration settings
• Basic technical logs used for system reliability and troubleshooting
WordPlayLeague does not collect private Slack messages or unrelated workspace data.
How We Use Information
Information collected by WordPlayLeague is used only to provide the core functionality of the service, including:
Recording puzzle scores submitted by players•
Generating league standings and leaderboards
Displaying statistics and historical results
Sending automated league updates within Slack
Supporting troubleshooting and system maintenance
We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
Data Retention
WordPlayLeague retains league data and player score history for as long as a league remains active in order to provide standings, statistics, and historical records.
If a league is deleted or a workspace removes the WordPlayLeague app, associated league data may be removed from our systems within a reasonable period of time.
Technical logs may be retained temporarily for security, operational, or troubleshooting purposes.
Data Access, Export, and Deletion Requests
Users may request access to, export of, or deletion of their personal data.
To submit a request, contact:
wordplayleague@gmail.com
Please include the Slack workspace name and league name associated with the request so we can locate the relevant data.
We will review and respond to requests within a reasonable timeframe.
Third-Party Services
WordPlayLeague integrates with third-party platforms such as Slack in order to operate league functionality. These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies.
Users should review the privacy policies of those services for more information.
Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the service or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page.
Contact
